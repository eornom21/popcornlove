Privacy

Make a Coloring Book (makeacoloringbook.com). Last updated November 27, 2026.

We keep this short because we collect very little. You don't need an account to use the site, and we never sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Things saved only on your device

Your favorites, the pages in “My book,” your print settings and the pages you make with “Draw my idea” (My Pages) are saved in your own browser. They never reach us. Clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or switching to another device or browser will lose them, so download a PDF of anything you want to keep.

Visit counts (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It sets cookies and records things like the pages you view, your browser and device type, and your rough location (city or country, not your street). Google Signals and ad personalization are turned off. If you're in Europe, Analytics loads only after you tap OK. Anywhere else, you can turn it off with Cookie choices, and we don't load it at all if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal.

Draw my idea

When you type an idea and tap “Draw it now,” the words you type are sent to OpenAI to draw the page. The finished picture comes back to your browser only; we don't keep it. To keep the free daily limit fair, our server keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address with a count of how many pages you made that day, and deletes it within two days. A Cloudflare Turnstile check runs first to make sure you're a person and not a bot. Please don't type names or personal details into your idea.

Page requests

If you use “Request a page,” the idea and difficulty level you choose go to a Google Form so we can draw it and add it to the site. We don't ask for your name or email there.

Email

If you email us at hello@makeacoloringbook.com, we use your message and address only to reply.

Our hosting

The site runs on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Like any web host, they process your internet address to deliver pages and stop abuse.

Children

Our pages are made for kids to color, but the site doesn't ask anyone for personal information, and we don't knowingly collect it from children under 13. If you think a child sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can change your cookie choice at any time with Cookie choices (also in the footer of every page), and you can clear what's saved on your device through your browser settings. Depending on where you live, including California and Europe, you may have the right to ask what we hold about you and to have it deleted. Since we hold almost nothing that identifies you, email us and we'll tell you what we have and remove it. We don't sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
