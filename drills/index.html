One-Touch Partner Passing

✓
Pumpkins 7/8 Warmup Mid · Late season

Run this season · Pumpkins 9/15

How it runs

  1. Partner up, start 5 or 6 feet apart.
  2. Pass back and forth without stopping the ball first.
  3. String several clean ones together, then both take a step back.
  4. If it falls apart, step back in and rebuild.

Keys

  • No trap — one touch straight back
  • Side of the foot, not the toe

Dribbling Maze

✓
Pumpkins 7/8 Dribbling Early · Mid season

A tetris-shaped cone path with square turns. Relay, then timed.

Run this season · Pumpkins 8/20

How it runs

  1. Lay cones in a tetris shape — straight runs with square turns, about 3 ft wide, in a 15x15 ft space.
  2. Dribble the whole shape without knocking a cone.
  3. Stop it with the sole at the end and hand off. Relay, next girl goes back the other way.
  4. Once everyone is clean, start timing and let them beat their best.

Keys

  • Small touches through the turns, longer on the straights
  • Eyes up so you see the next turn
  • Outside foot around the corners
  • Knock a cone and it does not count

Timed Passing and Trapping

–
Pumpkins 7/8 Passing Mid season

Not used yet this season

How it runs

  1. Rectangle about 7 ft wide by 15 ft long. Two girls at opposite ends.
  2. On GO, start a 45-second timer and count every successful pass and trap out loud.
  3. If the ball leaves the rectangle the round ends early.
  4. Each round they try to beat their best.

Keys

  • Side of the foot
  • Go fast, but keep enough control to stay in the box

Resize the box after the first rounds — it should be hard but not impossible.

Wall Passes

✓
Pumpkins 7/8 Passing Mid season

Beat a defender by playing it off a teammate and running past. Do not pass too early.

Run this season · Pumpkins 9/3

How it runs

  2. Attacker dribbles up from the defending goal line.
  3. Inside 5 yards of the defender she passes to the wall, runs past, calls, and shows her hand.

  • Call for it and show your hand

Soccer Islands

✓
Slimers 5/6Pumpkins 7/8 Passing Mid · Late season

Cone diamonds scattered down the field. You can only touch the ball while standing on an island.

Run this season · Pumpkins 8/20, 9/1  ·  Slimers 9/16

How it runs

  1. Scatter 8–10 islands down the field — 4 cones in a diamond about 5 ft across, 10–15 ft apart.
  2. The three islands nearest the goal you are attacking are RED.
  3. One girl starts on the island in front of our own goal with the ball. The rest start on the goal line.
  4. On GO, everyone runs to an island. You can only touch the ball on an island — everything else is water.
  5. Pass, then run to a new island. You can only shoot from a RED island.
  6. Ball leaves an island: bring it back to the last one and go again.

Keys

  • You cannot dribble your way out — pass and move
  • Get to an open island and CALL for it, loud
  • Look up before you pass — find the island, not just the player
  • Two girls never go to the same island
  • When the ball moves, you move

Timed version: start the clock on GO and stop it when the ball goes in. Let them beat their best.

Soccer Islands — Little Kid Version

✓
Slimers 5/6 Passing Mid season

Six big islands on a half field, one girl per island, no dribbling allowed at all.

Run this season · Slimers 9/16

How it runs

  1. 6 islands on one half — 4 cones in a square about 6 ft across, spread about 10 ft apart.
  2. Everybody starts on an island. One girl per island, nobody shares.
  3. You can only touch the ball standing on an island.
  4. Pass to a girl on another island, then run to a different empty one.
  5. Start with no goal — just count passes out loud. Add a goal once they have it.

Keys

  • One girl, one island
  • You cannot dribble — passing is the only way to move it
  • Yell her name before you pass
  • The second you pass, go find a new island

If the passes are too long, shrink the gaps or put a coach on an island as a helper.

Crossing — The Window

✓
Pumpkins 7/8 Shooting Late season

Cross from a marked window on the wing while two runners attack the front and back of the goal.

Run this season · Pumpkins 9/15

How it runs

  1. Two cones on the wing make a 'window', about even with the top of the box.
  2. Crosser starts at the half line on the sideline and dribbles down the line.
  3. Two runners start at the top of the box — one to the FRONT of the goal, one to the BACK.
  4. When the crosser reaches the window, she crosses. Runners time it to arrive with the ball.
  5. Rotate: crosser → back, back → front, front → end of line. Do both sides.

Keys

  • Cross when you reach the window, do not dribble into the corner
  • Aim for the spot in front of the goal, not at a person
  • Front runner goes early and hard
  • Back runner stays a step behind and wider — most crosses go long
  • One touch if you can get it

Pumpkins 7/8 Defending Early · Mid season

3 on 2 — Moving Before the Ball Gets There

✓
Pumpkins 7/8 Defending Late season

Two defenders shift with the ball as three attackers swing it wing to wing, then it goes live.

Run this season · Pumpkins 9/1

How it runs

  1. Three attackers spread across the field with the coach as the middle one. Two defenders.
  2. Before anything is live, the attackers pass the ball wing to wing and back. Defenders just move:
  3. · Ball to a wing — the defender on that side goes out to it
  4. · The other defender drops back and into the middle to cover behind her
  5. · Ball back in the middle — the two squeeze in together
  6. Once it has gone wing to wing and back, the coach starts the live attack from the middle.
  7. Go live until the defenders get a full clear past half. That is the only way the rep ends.

Keys

  • Move when the BALL moves, not when the player moves
  • One goes to the ball, one covers behind — never both at the ball
  • Ball in the middle, squeeze in together
  • TALK — call out who is going and who is covering
  • Not over until it is cleared past half

Soccer Choreography

✓
Pumpkins 7/8 Games Early · Mid · Late season

Small groups invent and then perform their own set play. The teaching happens while they argue about it.

Run this season · Pumpkins 9/3

How it runs

  1. Groups of 2, each sent to its own sideline — left and right on each half, four spots.
  2. Each group makes up a play that must have: at least 2 passes, somebody calling for the ball, somebody moving without the ball, and a goal.
  3. Work on it, then bring everyone in and have each group perform for the team.
  4. Then do it again in groups of 4 — same four requirements, bigger play.

Keys

  • Name your play — you have to be able to call it out
  • Everybody has a job, nobody just stands there
  • Call for the ball out loud, even in practice runs
  • Not finished until it ends in a goal

Let them be silly with it. The point is that they are talking to each other about where to move and why.
