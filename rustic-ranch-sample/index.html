The Venue

An old huller with a second life.

Before it hosted first dances, this building hulled almonds. The timber trusses, tin roof and wide concrete floor are all original. We added the lights, the bar and the comforts.

The result is a rare thing in the Valley: a covered open-air space big enough for a 500-person celebration, with shade at noon, a breeze in the evening and orchard rows running out in every direction. It's a blank canvas with character built in, and it photographs beautifully at any hour.

Family owned. Set right behind the family home.

The Huller

Bloom Season

For a few weeks each year, the orchard turns white and pink.

Almond bloom is the Valley's best-kept secret. The trees around the ranch open all at once, and for a short window the whole property is wrapped in blossoms. If your date lands in it, your photos will look like nowhere else.

JANFEBMARAPRMAY

Bloom typically peaks from mid-February into early March. Timing shifts with the weather each year, so we'll keep you posted as it approaches.

Bloom Packages

Good to Know

Questions, answered.

How many guests can Rustic Ranch hold?

The huller comfortably hosts events from about 50 guests up to 500, seated. We'll help you choose a layout that fits your headcount, dance floor and stage.

Is the venue indoors or outdoors?

Both, in the best way. The huller is fully roofed with open sides, so you get shade and weather cover with fresh air and orchard views.

Are restrooms included?

Yes. Two finished, private restrooms are built into the venue and included with every booking.
