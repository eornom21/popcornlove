# 2026 House Battlefield: Races to Watch
Compiled Thursday, Oct 29, 2026, five days before the Nov 3 general election. Facts are as of this week unless a row says otherwise.

**Source rule used.** Wikipedia, Ballotpedia and 270toWin were used only as leads. Every fact below cites a handicapper, a party committee, the FEC, a court, or a news outlet. Where I could not confirm something from such a source, the cell is blank or flagged.

## Read this first: caveats

1. **Cook's district-by-district ratings are paywalled.** Cook's free page ([cookpolitical.com/ratings/house-race-ratings](https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/house-race-ratings)) only defines the categories. I took the district placements from 270toWin's mirror of Cook's Oct 23 table, which is a lead and not a source. I then checked that mirror against news reports of Cook's changes:
   - [Forbes, Oct 23](https://www.forbes.com/sites/saradorn/2026/10/23/republicans-chances-of-maintaining-house-majority-have-all-but-evaporated-cook-political-report-says/): 17 Toss-ups, 12 of them R-held. The mirror matches exactly.
   - [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/map-shows-closest-house-races-as-cook-shifts-19-seats-toward-democrats-12611217): 16 Lean D and 17 Lean R. The mirror matches.
   - [Spotlight PA](https://www.spotlightpa.org/news/2026/10/scott-perry-janelle-stelson-10th-congressional-district-election-anti-corruption-campaign-finance-elections/): PA-10 moved from Toss-up to Lean D on Oct 9.
   - [Daily Montanan](https://dailymontanan.com/2026/10/17/analysts-shift-western-montana-congressional-district-to-toss-up-status/): MT-1 moved to Toss-up on Oct 16.
2. **Sabato.** The Oct 27 changes come directly from the [Crystal Ball (Oct 27)](https://sabatoscrystalball.substack.com/p/democrats-appear-to-hold-narrow-senate), which reports 17 Toss-ups, 225 seats at least leaning D and 193 at least leaning R. The full list of 17 is my own reconstruction: I applied those 16 changes to the Oct 20 table, and the result matches the 270toWin mirror for Oct 27. **The Crystal Ball says it will move every remaining Toss-up to a Lean before Election Day**, so expect one more update, probably Nov 2.
3. **Inside Elections.** The latest issue I could find is **Oct 15, 2026 (Vol. 10, No. 20)** ([PDF copy of the newsletter](https://www.americanwaterways.com/sites/default/files/AWO%2010-15-2026.pdf); changes reported by [Roll Call, Oct 20](https://rollcall.com/2026/10/20/rating-changes-democrats-inside-elections/)). Inside Elections publishes about every two weeks, so a new issue may come out Oct 29 or 30. Inside Elections also uses "Tilt" ratings, which fall between Toss-up and Lean.
4. **CA-13 has a conflicting Cook rating.** Cook's own race page shows "Lean D", last updated Jan 15, 2026 ([Cook race page](https://www.cookpolitical.com/house/race/481881)), and [CalMatters, Oct 16](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/10/california-election-congressional-district-13-gray/) also reports the January move to Lean D. But the 270toWin mirror of Cook's Oct 23 table lists CA-13 as **Likely D**. Check Cook's site before saying either one on stage.

**Net effect:**
- **Nine states have new maps in force for November:** TX, CA, NC, OH, UT, FL, LA, TN and AL. Missouri's and Virginia's new maps were blocked in court.
- **On paper the maps tilt Republican.** The [Washington Examiner, Oct 9](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/congressional/4761193/gop-seats-new-congressional-maps-2026-elections/) says race raters project Republican gains of 2 to 5 seats from the GOP maps, against up to 6 Democratic gains from California and Utah.
- **In practice it may come close to a wash.** [Decision Desk HQ (Sept 14)](https://decisiondeskhq.substack.com/p/redistricting-congress-republican-majority-2026-midterms-forecast-democrats-trump-gerrymander-california-texas-florida-ohio-north-carolina-callais) forecast that redistricting nets roughly 2 seats toward Democrats once the national environment is factored in. The reason is that a "dummymander" spreads GOP voters too thin in a wave year.

### CA-13: Adam Gray (D, incumbent) vs Kevin Lincoln (R) — *the home-district race*
**Dynamics:**
- **One of the closest seats in the country, twice.** Gray won in 2024 by **187 votes** over John Duarte. Duarte had beaten Gray by 564 votes in 2022 ([CalMatters 10/16](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/10/california-election-congressional-district-13-gray/)).
- **Prop 50 redrew it.** The district now runs from Stockton down Highway 99 to Merced and has a Democratic advantage on paper.
- **The challenger is a party pick.** Lincoln is a former Stockton mayor and a Marine veteran, chosen for the NRCC's MAGA Majority program on Mar 17. Gray is on the DCCC Frontline list.

**Fundraising (FEC, through Oct 14):**
- Gray: $5,241,028 raised; $848,633 COH ([FEC](https://www.fec.gov/data/candidate/H2CA13115/))
- Lincoln: $2,971,922 raised; $448,388 COH ([FEC](https://www.fec.gov/data/candidate/H4CA09093/))

[ABC30, Oct 29](https://abc30.com/post/adam-gray-maintains-fundraising-edge-californias-13th-congressional-district-race/19992058/) reports Gray at more than $5M and Lincoln at $2.89M. Its COH figures ($1.2M and $682K) do not match the FEC's Oct 14 numbers, so use the FEC's.

**Where the money comes from** (useful for the talk), per ABC30: most money for both campaigns came from California. Gray drew $278K from New York and $224K from Massachusetts. Lincoln drew $278K from Texas and about $225K from Florida.

**Outside spending:**
- Congressional Leadership Fund (GOP): more than **$4.34M** as of Oct 11.
- House Majority PAC (Dem): **under $400K** ([CalMatters](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/10/california-election-congressional-district-13-gray/)).
